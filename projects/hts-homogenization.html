HTS Homogenization & ROM

Homogenization methods and reduced-order hysteretic models for high-temperature superconducting windings and composite conductors.

Overview

Full 3D finite element models of HTS windings are computationally expensive. This project develops homogenization techniques and reduced-order magnetization models to accelerate simulations while preserving essential physics of composite superconductors.

Key contributions

  • Reduced-order hysteretic magnetization and flux models
  • Finite element a-h formulations for vector hysteresis in composites
  • Strand and cable homogenization for magnet-scale simulations
