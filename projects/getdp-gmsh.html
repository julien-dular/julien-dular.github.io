GetDP & Gmsh

Contributions to widely used open-source tools for mesh generation and variational finite element solving.

Overview

Gmsh is a flexible mesh generator; GetDP is a general finite element solver based on differential forms. Together they form the backbone of many superconductivity simulation workflows developed in Liège and at CERN.

Contributions

  • Superconductor formulations and workshops (GetDP school at CERN)
  • Helicoidal transformations and periodic meshing for twisted conductors
  • Reproducible pipelines linking Gmsh meshes to GetDP models
