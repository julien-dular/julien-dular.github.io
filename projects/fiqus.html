CERN · Electro-thermal simulation

FiQuS

Modern simulation framework for electro-thermal transients in accelerator magnets at CERN.

Overview

FiQuS (Field Quench Simulation) targets multiphysics modelling of superconducting accelerator magnets, coupling electromagnetic fields with thermal effects during operation and quench events.

Contributions

  • Development of novel finite element methods for magnet simulation
  • Homogenization and reduced-order models for composite conductors
  • Integration with open-source meshing and solvers used at CERN

Technologies

  • Finite elements
  • GetDP
  • Gmsh
  • Python
