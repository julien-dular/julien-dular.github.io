Rutherford Cable Modelling

Finite element models of inter-strand coupling currents, AC loss, and magnetization in Rutherford cables for accelerator magnets.

Overview

Rutherford cables used in LHC-type magnets exhibit complex eddy-current and coupling-current phenomena. This project develops distributed FE models and the coupled axial–transverse currents (CATC) method to capture strand-level physics at cable scale.

Key contributions

  • Distributed inter-strand coupling current model for Rutherford cables
  • Coupled axial and transverse currents method for periodic superconductors
  • Simulation of AC loss and magnetization under operating conditions
